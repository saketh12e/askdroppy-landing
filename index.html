Made for the place

Inside Droppy, Ask Droppy is quiet. Dark chrome, native type, flat washes, and the host's own springs. These specimens follow the DroppyKit guidelines.

What's real, and what isn't

Droppy today
Notch and island surfaces, shelf widgets, live activities, and 39 droplets, including AI tools.
DroppyKit supports
Shelf widgets, live activities, expanded surfaces (up to two-thirds of the display), HUDs, settings panes, and MCP tools.
Proposed
Ask Droppy itself, including voice, context from selections, and the detached companion workspace. Those features would need native work beyond today's SDK.
On this page
Every interaction is simulated in the browser with prewritten content.
